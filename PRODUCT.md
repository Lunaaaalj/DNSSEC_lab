# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single folder at `docs/actividad3.1.1/`, keyboard-navigable, printable to PDF. No framework.

## Users

University cryptography/security course (same course as `docs/actividad2`). Audience: classmates and a professor/grader, in a classroom, projected. Presenter is the student delivering in English (changed from Spanish after the first mock round).

## Product Purpose

A presentation deck for Actividad 3.1.1: pentesting methods that use social engineering. It must show command of the methodology (phases, techniques, defenses) and include a live demo or case study. Success: the grader sees rigor and clear structure; classmates follow and remember the core idea.

## Positioning

An academic deliverable from a hands-on lab repository (DNSSEC lab, BIND, TShark, Wireshark evidence). The demo/case must be an authorized, simulated scenario, not an attack on real people or organizations.

## Operating Context

Projected in a classroom; read from a distance. Delivered in English. Likely timed (short slot). Previous activity used a Typst report with captured screenshots as evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The user's earlier decks (`stochastic_refinit/docs/presentacion`, `geostats/docs/presentacion`) set the bar and are binding as a quality reference: one persistent world behind every slide that a camera moves across; animation that explains the reasoning; a single star moment; keyboard navigation (arrows/space, `O` index, `N` notes, `F` fullscreen); slide position in the URL hash; hover/step-revealed detail; self-contained, works offline; printable to PDF with reduced motion. Visual style is bespoke per deck and tied to its subject, "profound" rather than template.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English.
- Static HTML deck, no build step required.
- Demo/case content is synthetic and labeled as such; no real targets, credentials, or personal data.
- Exact slide count, slot length, and case details are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

Repo contains prior lab evidence under `docs/actividad2/` (screenshots, report). No social-engineering evidence, screenshots, or case material exists yet; none may be fabricated as real.

## Product Principles

- Authorized, simulated, labeled: every demonstration is synthetic and says so.
- Legible from the back of a classroom before it is clever.
- Method first: phases and defenses structure the story, not tool names.
- Show the human mechanism (trust, urgency, authority), not only the tooling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Projector-grade contrast and large type; no information carried by color alone; reduced-motion respected.
